# Contributing

Pintura is early, and its direction can still change.

Issues are welcome: bugs, decks that export wrong, and slides the format can't express.
There are no promises on timing.

Open an issue before a pull request, so we agree on the change before you write it.
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the vocabulary and conventions, and [Changing Pintura](README.md#changing-pintura) covers running from source.
As section 5 of the [license](LICENSE) says, a contribution you submit is licensed under Apache-2.0.

Forks are welcome, and may keep the name and logo; see [NOTICE](NOTICE).
